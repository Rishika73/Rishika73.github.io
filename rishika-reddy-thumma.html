Real-time · Online store data

Real-Time E-Commerce Data Platform

Streaming / CDC

What it does: The moment anything changes in an online store — a new order, a payment, a refund — this system captures it and updates the reporting data within seconds, instead of waiting for an overnight batch.

OutcomeReports always show the store as it is right now — and every change passes 8 automatic error checks, with a full history kept of everything that ever changed.

How it works

Store database→ Change captured instantly→ Streamed & processed live→ Checked for errors→ Ready-to-use reports

Analytics · Revenue & customer risk

Customer Revenue & Risk Platform

Warehouse / BI

What it does: It pulls customer and billing data into one place, keeps a full history of how each customer changes over time, and scores every customer for health and for how likely they are to leave.

OutcomeOne live dashboard shows $2.91M in annual recurring revenue and flags the 2 customers most at risk of leaving — so the team knows exactly where to act first. You can open it below.

Customer & billing files→ Loaded automatically→ Cleaned & scored→ Tested for errors→ Live dashboard

03

Lakehouse · Retail sales data

Databricks Retail Lakehouse

Lakehouse

What it does: It takes raw retail sales data and refines it in three stages — raw, cleaned, ready-to-use — building a single trustworthy view of each customer, each segment, and each day's sales.

OutcomeBad data never reaches a report: 6 automatic quality checks gate every run, and faulty records are quarantined — in the sample data they caught 3 bad orders out of 17 before those could distort the sales numbers.

How it works

Raw sales data→ Cleaned & de-duplicated→ Bad records quarantined→ Quality checks pass→ Sales dashboard
